How I work

Four habits that show up in everything I ship.

Frame

Start from the constraint

The real brief is usually hiding under the stated one. I look for it before opening Figma.

Cut

Remove before adding

Every screen gets a pass just to ask what can leave without anyone noticing.

Prototype

Feel it before it's real

Static comps hide timing problems. I build interactions early, even roughly.

Ship

Stay in it past handoff

Design isn't done at delivery — I sit with engineering through the edge cases.
